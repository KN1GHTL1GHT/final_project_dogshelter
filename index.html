How long do Austin's shelter dogs wait?

Each dot is one dog's stay at the shelter, from the day it came in to the day it left. The higher the dot, the longer the wait. Scroll to sort the same dogs in different ways.

    Sorted by coat color

    Dogs are lined up by the main coat color the shelter wrote down. Height shows days in the shelter, on a stretched scale so the many short stays don't pile into one corner.

    Look for the short line

    The dark line in each column is the median: half of that group left sooner, half waited longer. Hover or tap a dot to meet the dog.

    Now sorted by age

    Same dogs, same heights. Each dot only slides sideways into a column for how old the dog was on the day it arrived. Dots keep their coat color so you can follow them.

    Data: Austin Animal Center Outcomes, City of Austin open data portal. A random sample of 1,000 dog and puppy stays.
